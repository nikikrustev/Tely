Part 1

The first six, explained simply

The ideas from the shortlist, each with a kid-level explanation added. TwinCast is still the recommended build.

ATwinCast

Like you're 5We make a pretend football game inside the computer, like a video game. Then a robot watches it and says what happened. We made the game, so we know the right answers and can check the robot's work, like a teacher marking a test.

BSpeed Trap

Like you're 5When a player runs super, super fast, a little name sign pops up on TV saying how fast they went, and why that run was important.

COne Number

Like you're 5You pick your favourite thing to count, like “how fast” or “how many passes”, and the TV tells you the whole game using just that one number.

DChalk Talk

Like you're 5The man on TV asks a question, and the computer draws arrows and circles on the picture, like drawing on a window with a marker, to show the answer.

ERealism Bench

Like you're 5Lots of people make pretend football games. We make a checker that says “this looks like real football” or “this looks silly”.

FGraphics Desk

Like you're 5The computer suggests pictures to put on the TV, and a grown-up presses “yes” or “no” before anyone sees them.
Part 2

Best three from the new batch

None of these replaces TwinCast. Each one either bolts onto it or is a fallback that is cheap to build and easy to show in a 60-second video.

01

Ghost Team

The most visual idea here and nobody in the field does it. It reuses TwinCast's renderer and simulator: the ghost is just the simulator's “average” run drawn on top of the real one.
21

Called It

40+ entries say “trust us, we fact-check”. This one shows a track record instead. Cheap to build, and the calibration chart is a strong closing shot for the demo.
10

Story Time

A third audience the brief doesn't name but obviously invites. Easy to build, instantly understood by judges, and it gives the demo a warm, memorable moment.

All 24 new ideas

01

Ghost Team

On screen
OpenEffort MTwinCast mode

A see-through “average team” plays alongside the real one, so when a player does something unusual you see the gap between them and the ghost.

Like you're 5In racing games you can see a see-through ghost car. We put a ghost football team on the TV that does the normal thing. When a real player does something clever, you can see it, because they're not where the ghost is.
Brief hook
Explain (why a moment matters) and Render, drawn on the picture itself.
Nearest in the field
No direct match. 126312 Second Guess and 126528 Fulcrum do counterfactuals, but as numbers, not a ghost on screen.
02

Decoy Credit

On screen
Lightly takenEffort S–MTwinCast mode

Finds the runs that drag defenders away so a teammate gets free, and gives the runner on-screen credit.

Like you're 5Sometimes a player runs away just to trick the other team, so their friend can get the ball. Nobody claps for that. We draw a glowing line so everyone sees the trick and says thank you.
Brief hook
Interpret and Explain; works as part of player-focus mode.
Nearest in the field
126392 GhostPlay AI (off-ball support, xT) and 126359 Shield (invisible defensive work). Neither turns decoy runs into a broadcast moment.
03

Dive Judges

On screen
Lightly takenEffort STwinCast mode

Pass difficulty shown the way Olympic diving is scored: a judging panel, a degree of difficulty and a score, popping up after special passes.

Like you're 5Some passes are easy, like rolling the ball to a friend next to you. Some are super hard, like throwing a paper ball into a bin across the room. Pretend judges hold up scorecards to show how hard it was.
Brief hook
Brief ii-b, pass quality: distance, accuracy, difficulty rating.
Nearest in the field
126447 xPlain Live and 126684 MatchLens compute pass difficulty. The metric exists; the scorecard presentation doesn't.
04

Duel Card

On screen
OpenEffort M

Tracks the key one-on-one battle (say, winger vs full-back) through the match as a round-by-round boxing scorecard.

Like you're 5Two players keep racing each other all game, like two kids always racing to the swings. We keep score of who wins each race, like in boxing, and show who's ahead.
Brief hook
Interpret, player focus, and a running narrative.
Nearest in the field
No direct match. 126697 MatchLens per-player dashboards come closest, but they track one player, not a matchup.
05

Set-Piece X-Ray

On screen
OpenEffort MTwinCast mode

Before a corner or free kick, predicts the routine and who will mark whom, then grades its own guess once the ball is kicked.

Like you're 5Before a corner kick, everyone has a secret plan for where to run. We guess the plan and draw it on the TV. Then we watch and see if we guessed right.
Brief hook
Explain and Render. Needs set-piece routines in the generator, which also counts as data innovation (v).
Nearest in the field
None of the 332 makes set pieces the focus.
06

Relay Credit

On screen
Lightly takenEffort S

Every goal is traced back through the whole move, and the credit is split among everyone who touched it, shown as a relay-race graphic.

Like you're 5A goal is like a relay race. The last kid crosses the line, but everyone carried the baton. We give a sticker to every kid who helped, not just the last one.
Brief hook
Explain and Narrative (player-performance context).
Nearest in the field
126704 Why That Goal? rewinds the build-up; 126361 Shift11 credits momentum to players. Neither shares out a goal.
07

Déjà Vu

On screen
OpenEffort M

Spots when a move repeats something from earlier in the same match and says what happened last time: “third time this switch has worked”.

Like you're 5Your friend always hides behind the same tree in hide and seek. We notice when a team does the same trick again and tell you, “Watch out, they did this before!”
Brief hook
Explain (game-rhythm patterns) and Narrative.
Nearest in the field
126635 The Metronome detects shape changes; 126386 Second Story looks at what the match was becoming. Neither matches repeated moves.
08

Weather Player

On screen
OpenEffort S–M

Adds rain, wind and a heavy pitch to the synthetic data, and explains when the weather, not the players, changed the game.

Like you're 5When it rains, the ball gets slippy and passes go wrong. The weather is like a sneaky extra player. We tell you when it's the rain making the mess, not the players.
Brief hook
Data innovation (v) and Explain.
Nearest in the field
Nobody in the field models weather.
09

Keeper Brain

On screen
OpenEffort MTwinCast mode

A goalkeeper-only mode: positioning, angles, shots faced versus expected, and the saves that didn't need a dive because the keeper was already in the right place.

Like you're 5The goalkeeper is the only one allowed to use their hands. The best ones stand in exactly the right spot so they don't even need to jump. We draw lines to show the clever standing.
Brief hook
Player-focus mode and Explain.
Nearest in the field
Nobody in the 332 builds for goalkeepers.
10

Story Time

Fans
Lightly takenEffort S

A kids' mode: the match retold as a short story with characters, simple words and pictures, for under-10s watching with their family.

Like you're 5The football game turns into a bedtime story. The players are the characters, and the goal is the exciting bit at the end.
Brief hook
Personalise: a third audience beyond analyst and casual fan. Works in any language.
Nearest in the field
126724 Pizarra, 126648 Coach in Your Ear and 126440 One Match, Many Matches target newcomers. None aim at children.
11

Player Diary

Fans
Lightly takenEffort S

A first-person recap from the player you follow: “I ran 11.2 km. I faded after 70 minutes. My best moment was…”

Like you're 5After the game, your favourite player writes in their diary about their day: what went well, when they got tired, and their best bit.
Brief hook
Player-focus mode and match recaps.
Nearest in the field
126646 Mauled by the Tigers (player cam and personal recap, but only a short write-up).
12

Jargon Buster

Fans
Lightly takenEffort M

Tap any football word the commentator uses (“low block”, “half-space”) and get a five-second animation built from this match.

Like you're 5Grown-ups use big football words. You tap the word, and a tiny cartoon from the game shows you what it means.
Brief hook
Explain, Render and Personalise for newcomers.
Nearest in the field
126648 Coach in Your Ear teaches new fans; 126615 Matchday Explainer explains the rules. Neither uses the live match as the example.
13

Recap Dial

Fans
CrowdedEffort S

One slider sets the recap length, from 15 seconds to 10 minutes, and the story is re-cut for that length, not just trimmed.

Like you're 5You tell us how much time you have, a tiny bit or a lot, and we tell you the story of the game in exactly that much time.

Narrative (recaps) and Personalise.
Nearest in the field

Like you're 5TVs are wide, but phones are tall. We cut the picture so it fits your phone and always keeps the ball in the middle.
